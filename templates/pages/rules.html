Rules

Snowball is one shared room for any conversation, run by one person. These are the few things that get something removed. When something is removed, the reason shows in its place, and every removal is listed in the moderation log.

No spam

Ads, scams, link farms, and the same thing posted over and over. Sharing something you made is fine; turning Snowball into a billboard isn't.

No harassment

Argue with ideas as hard as you like. Attacking, threatening, or hounding a person is out, whether they're on Snowball or not.

No personal information

Snowball is anonymous, and that only works if it stays that way for everyone. Don't post anything that could identify someone: names, addresses, workplaces, photos, accounts elsewhere. That includes your own.

Nothing illegal

Nothing that's against the law to post or to host. Anything that sexualizes minors is removed, and reported to the authorities.

Use some sense

Tag things honestly, keep it about the topic, and don't game the votes. Anything that's plainly against the spirit of the place may be removed as "breaking the rules".

Reporting

If you see something that breaks these rules, use the "report" link under it. Nobody can see who reported what, not even the admin. Something reported by several people for illegal content or personal information is hidden until the admin has looked at it.
