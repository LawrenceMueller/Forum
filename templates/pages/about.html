About Snowball

A small, anonymous forum for any conversation, built and run by one person who got tired of the modern web. No ads, no tracking, no algorithm deciding what you see, and no little chat bubbles.

How it works

Anyone can read. With an account you can post (a link, some text, or both), comment, and vote. Every post is filed under one to three topics from a shared tree of tags, so a post about sourdough also shows up under Bread, Baking, and Food & drink. The front page shows what's well liked today and the conversations that are active right now; everything else is sorted by plain old time.

What Snowball knows about you

As little as it can, on purpose.

  • No email, no phone number, no real name. Signing up asks for nothing about you. Your username is generated for you, so it can't give you away, and it can't be changed.
  • Your password is never stored, only a slow, salted hash of it. If you forget it, your recovery codes are the only way back in. There's no email to reset it with.
  • No IP addresses are stored or logged. They're held in memory for a few minutes to slow down abuse, then forgotten.
  • Votes aren't tied to your account. They're stored under a scrambled fingerprint, so nobody, the admin included, can list who voted for what. Snowball doesn't even record when you voted.
  • Reports are anonymous, even to the admin.
  • No tracking, analytics, ads, or third-party anything. Every page, script, and font comes from Snowball itself. Snowball is also kept out of search engines.
  • One cookie, to keep you logged in. Logging out logs you out everywhere.

What it keeps, and for how long

  • Your posts and comments, under your generated name, until you delete them.
  • Deleting erases. When you delete a post or comment, its words are wiped from the database, not just hidden. A deleted post keeps its title, so the discussion under it still makes sense.
  • You can delete your whole account at any time, and choose to erase everything you wrote along with it.
  • Things the admin removes keep their text for 30 days (for appeals and mistakes), then it's erased. Handled reports are deleted after 30 days too.
  • Your private history and replies pages show what you've written, voted on, and been replied to. Only you can see them.

Who runs it

One person, in their spare time. Moderation follows the rules, and every removal is listed in the public moderation log, without content or names. News about the site itself goes in the notes. There's no email address to write to: questions, ideas, and bug reports go under the Meta tag.
